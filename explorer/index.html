Decision search

Filters use exact controlled values. Every row retains stable identifiers and official evidence links.

Comparable cases

Not released yet. Nearest-case comparisons require a reviewed full corpus, grouped model validation, and the frozen minimum-support threshold. The interface will not rank provisional pilot cases or manufacture a model result.

The released view will show observed context differences, expected outcome, residual, support score, model version, and an unavailable-evidence warning.

Competitive impact

Mechanical arithmetic is displayed separately from strategy-dependent or non-estimable effects.

Victim harm and lasting consequences

Observed harm, damage evidence, forced stops, and modeled persistent pace loss remain separate from responsibility and sanction burden. The table is a proportionality review input, not an automatic verdict that a penalty was wrong.

Incident location and causal context

Turn ranges and directed multi-car edges preserve official context without assigning blame to every participant.

Carried-over sanction effects

Exact starting-grid displacement is separated from uncertain race-finish and points counterfactuals.
