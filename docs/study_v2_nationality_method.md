# Study v2 Nationality Gate

Nationality is a secondary diagnostic. The frozen gate requires at least 98 British-accused cases,
90% common support, a maximum overlap-weighted absolute standardized difference of 0.10, an exposed
effective sample size of 30, 80% simulated power at the target difference, and completed independent
review.

The current 346-case design has 44 British-accused cases. Measured overlap is usable: common support
is 97.7%, exposed effective sample size is 43.1, and the maximum weighted imbalance is 0.039. The
sample and power gates fail. At a 15-point target difference, simulated power ranges from 37.8% to
53.6%. Independent review is also incomplete.

The pipeline therefore publishes raw group composition and outcome-free design diagnostics only.
It does not fit or release an adjusted nationality effect. If every gate later passes, event-grouped
inference, sensitivity exclusions, and Benjamini-Hochberg correction remain required.
