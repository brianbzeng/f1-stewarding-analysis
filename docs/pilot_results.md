## What the pilot established

- FIA document titles and layouts vary materially across 2019, 2023, and 2025, but a versioned,
  label-aware parser can preserve the core evidentiary sections.
- FIA occasionally advertises recalled records without a downloadable link. They must remain in the
  lineage table even though their content cannot be checksummed.
- A literal percent sign in one FIA filename produced an invalid transport URL. The collector now
  preserves valid percent-encoded bytes and escapes only stray percent signs.
- Re-running one event must merge the manifest and recover existing verified downloads; it must not
  replace the multi-event inventory or erase retrieval metadata.
- An archive-wide decision list contains many strict-liability and administrative offences. The
  primary driving-incident queue should be generated by declared inclusion rules, not memory.
- FIA regulation archives can exhibit version drift. The 2023 Appendix L entry is dated 19 October,
  but its current download is a 6 December revision published after Abu Dhabi; it is therefore
  registered as metadata-only pending recovery of the event-date binary.
- Official incident times are minute-rounded and a lapped driver's personal lap can differ from the
  global race lap. Lap coding must reconcile the written reason, Race Control, and timing rather
  than accept an automated nearest-lap suggestion as fact.
- Competitive impact must be tiered. The two post-race-added time penalties can be re-ranked
  mechanically; a served penalty and a next-event grid penalty cannot.

## Remaining independent-review gate

The nine candidate rows cover paired 2019 Austria decisions, two 2023 Abu Dhabi collision decisions,
and five 2025 Austria decisions involving collisions or forcing a driver off track. An AI-assisted
first pass has coded their incident grouping, final outcome, counterpart, lap/turn, evidence,
guideline conformance, and primary-study inclusion. An independent reviewer must confirm or correct
each field and reconcile disagreements. Recalled 2025 documents 29 and 39 are preserved as archive
records but were unavailable for content review.

## Scale recommendation

The technical pilot supports continued method and report development. Do not collect all 2018–2025
events until independent review estimates minutes per record, tests the controlled vocabulary, and
confirms that recalled/corrected lineage can be resolved with an acceptable unresolved rate.
